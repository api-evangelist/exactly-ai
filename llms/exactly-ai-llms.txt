# exactly.ai

> exactly.ai is a private AI image platform for brands and creative teams that need bespoke, on-brand visual generation, editing, and style transfer at scale.

exactly.ai helps brands and creative teams turn existing brand visuals into reusable private styles, generate new assets that stay within brand rules, and scale production across marketing, product, and creative workflows. The platform supports both custom-trained style models and base models for broader image generation, alongside editing, upscaling, vectorization, animation, and API-based integration.

**Important notes:**
- Private styles are trained on a customer's own assets and are private by default — no one else can access or use them.
- Training data is not shared with third parties and is not reused to train global or public models.
- All uploaded images, trained models, and generated outputs belong to the creator.
- Rights are model-dependent: private-style and artist-style outputs require a license for commercial use and export; base-model rights depend on the underlying provider.
- SOC 2 Type II compliant.

## Core capabilities
- **Style transfer**: train a custom AI model from as few as 10 reference images to replicate any visual style and generate on-brand images at scale.
- **Base model (e.gent)**: photorealistic image generation from text prompts — no style training required.
- **Character and product pinning**: lock signature characters or products consistently across hundreds of styled assets.
- **Editing tools**: inpainting, outpainting, background removal, image-to-image variations.
- **Animation**: generate motion from still images with keyframes and timeline controls.
- **SVG vectorization** of generated assets.
- **Up to 8K image upscaling**.
- **Multilingual prompt support**.
- **Enterprise API**: integrate style transfer and generation into your own products.
